YUCCA

    Play with the controller you like, on a Wii or a Switch.

    YUCCA turns a small ESP32 board into a wireless adapter. Your controller talks to the board, and the board acts like a controller your console already knows.

    Which console do you have?

    PlayStation 4 and 5 come later.

    Which controller will you use?

    One at a time. You can switch later.

    Put YUCCA on the board

    Plug the board into this computer, then click Install. You only do this once.

    • An ESP32 board. Called "ESP32-WROOM-32" or "ESP32 DevKit" in shops. S3, C3 and C6 boards won't work.
    • A USB cable that carries data. Some cables only charge.
    • Chrome or Edge on a computer.

    This browser can't install YUCCA. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a Windows, Mac, Linux or ChromeOS computer.

    Checking for the latest version…

    Keep the board plugged in.

    YUCCA is installed.

    My board isn't in the list

    Your computer may need a driver for the board's USB chip: CP210x or CH340. The chip's name is printed next to the USB port. Restart the browser afterwards, or try another cable.

    It can't connect to the board

    Hold the BOOT button on the board, click Install YUCCA again, and let go once the progress bar appears.

    Connect to your adapter

    Keep the board plugged into this computer. If a list appears, pick the board.
