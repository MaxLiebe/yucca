YUCCA

Your Universal Cross-platform Controller Adapter. Play with almost any Bluetooth controller on your console, through an ESP32.

Your browser can't talk to USB serial devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

1 Install

Plug in an original ESP32 board (ESP32-WROOM-32 or WROVER; the S3, C3 and C6 can't talk to a Wii or Switch) with a data USB cable, then:

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer to install. Open this page over https to install.

Checking for the latest release…

Nothing in the list? Install the USB driver for your board (CP210x or CH340), or hold the BOOT button while you click Install.

2 Settings

Choose your console and options. Close any other serial monitor first.

3 Pair

  1. Put your controller in pairing mode (how, per controller).
  2. The first time, YUCCA then opens the console's pairing window by itself. On a Wii or Wii U press the red SYNC button; on a Switch open Controllers → Change Grip/Order.
  3. Afterwards, turning the controller on is enough.

No buttons needed on the ESP32: hold Select + Home for 3 s to pick the console from the controller, or Select + Start for 3 s to pair with a console again.
