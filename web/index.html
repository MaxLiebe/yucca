Play with the controller you like, on a Wii or a Switch.

YUCCA turns a small ESP32 board into a wireless adapter. Your controller talks to the board, and the board acts like a controller your console already knows. You set it up right here, in your browser.

Put YUCCA on the board

You do this once. It takes a minute or two.

You'll need

  • An ESP32 board. Look for "ESP32-WROOM-32" or "ESP32 DevKit" when you buy one. Newer boards called S3, C3 or C6 won't work, because they can't talk to a Wii or Switch.
  • A USB cable that carries data. Some cables only charge. If your board doesn't show up, try another cable.
  • Chrome or Edge on a computer. Phones, tablets and Safari can't install it.

This browser can't install YUCCA. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a Windows, Mac, Linux or ChromeOS computer.

  1. Plug the board into this computer.
  2. Click Install YUCCA. A small window opens with a list. Pick your board (it's often called "CP2102", "CH340" or "USB Serial") and click Connect.
  3. Choose Install. If it asks to erase the board, say yes. Keep the board plugged in until it says it's done.
Open this page over https to install.

The list is empty, or my board isn't in it

Your computer may need a driver for the board's USB chip. Look at the small chip next to the USB port, then install the driver that matches: CP210x or CH340. Restart the browser afterwards.

Still nothing? Try a different cable or USB port.

It gets stuck on "Connecting"

Hold the BOOT button on the board, click Install YUCCA again, and let go once the progress bar starts moving.
